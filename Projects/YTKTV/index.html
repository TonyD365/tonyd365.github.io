YTKTV

Turn a computer and a TV into a karaoke room.

Every song on YouTube is in the song book. The TV shows the music video with karaoke lyrics, AI takes the singer's voice out, guests queue from their phones, and a mixing console puts your microphones on top.

cat why.txt

Why I made it

I wanted to sing karaoke at home, and the only way was YouTube in a browser: ads between songs, a page made for watching, and none of the atmosphere of a real KTV. The open-source apps I tried looked nothing like a KTV, were limited to their own library, or had stopped being maintained. So I built my own in Python.

ytktv --features

What it does

songs
Search YouTube, paste a link, or browse hot songs. Pinyin initials work too.
vocals
AI separation in the background, on your GPU when there is one, with a gapless switch.
lyrics
Synced karaoke lyrics from captions or LRCLIB, or written by Whisper on-device.
phones
Scan a QR code to queue songs with no app. A phone can even be a microphone.
mixer
EQ, compressor, echo, reverb, a noise gate, and a feedback guard on each channel.
scoring
A live pitch lane, scores out of 100, duets, and PK battles.
party
Fourteen games, danmaku, crowd sounds, mood scenes, and smart lights.

ytktv-desk --about

YTKTV Desk

A staff terminal for venues with more than one room. It handles what a real KTV front desk does, and its links to the rooms are secured with lanpair.

  • Open, extend, and close rooms with timers
  • Service calls, orders, bills, and receipts
  • Members, packages, coupons, and bookings
  • Reports on takings, busy hours, and top songs

install ytktv

Try it

Windows:

winget install TonyD365.YTKTV

macOS and Linux:

curl -fsSL https://tonyd365.github.io/YTKTV/install.sh | bash

YTKTV is free to use, including in a business, but the software may not be sold. Playing music in a public venue normally needs licences from the rights holders. It is an independent project, not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
